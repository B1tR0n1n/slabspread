House Edge Index

For each pack a platform sells, the expected buyback value of a pull against the price of the pack: house_edge = 1 − Σ pᵢ × buybackᵢ / price. Negative means the guaranteed buyback is worth more than the pack; positive is what the house keeps. Tier values are the midpoints of the platform's published value bands; buyback is the platform's stated percentage of that. Full definitions and a worked example: methodology.
