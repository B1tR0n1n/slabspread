Valuation Gap

For every card with both a platform-stated value and at least three real sales in the last 30 days, we take the ratio platform value ÷ median real sale. This page shows the distribution of that ratio per platform — never an individual card's value. A median above 1.00 means the platform values its inventory above what the same cards actually trade for. Platforms with fewer than five such cards are omitted.
