## How many requests will you actually use?

Most people over-buy because they budget per card instead of per call. One batch request can carry up to 200 cards, so the arithmetic is usually kinder than it looks.

A collection tracker

5,000 tracked cards refreshed once a day, batched 100 at a time, is 50 requests a day. That fits inside Starter with room to spare.

A storefront repricing nightly

A 20,000-card catalogue repriced every night in 100-card batches is 200 requests a day, roughly 6,000 a month. Starter covers it; Professional gives you headroom to reprice more often.

A market scanner

Scanners are usually bounded by the per-minute limit rather than the monthly one. If you are sweeping sets on a loop, size on requests per minute first.

Usage is visible in your dashboard in real time, and every response carries your remaining allowance. If you outgrow a plan mid-month, upgrading takes effect immediately. See the rate limits documentation for reset times and retry guidance.

## Pricing questions, answered

The things developers ask us before they pick a plan. No reading check required.

What counts as a request?

One HTTP call to a JustTCG endpoint is one request, whatever it returns. A batch POST /cards that fetches 100 cards still costs a single request, which is why batching is the cheapest way to reprice a large inventory.

Is the free tier really free?

Yes, and it needs no credit card. You get 1,000 requests a month, capped at 100 a day and 10 a minute, with access to every game and every endpoint. The free tier is for personal and non-commercial use; shipping a commercial product means moving to a paid plan.

What happens when I hit a limit?

Exceeding the per-minute limit returns 429 Too Many Requests; back off and retry. Exceeding your monthly allowance means upgrading to continue. Nothing is silently truncated and nothing overage-bills you by surprise.

Is Enterprise unlimited?

No. Enterprise is metered like every other tier, at 500,000 requests a month, 50,000 a day, and 500 a minute. What it adds is headroom, 200-card batches, priority routing, and direct support. If you need more than that, talk to us.

Do the prices include tax?

No. All prices are in USD and exclusive of sales tax. Tax is calculated at checkout based on your billing location, so the total you pay depends on where you are.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Any time, from your dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately so you are not stuck waiting out a billing cycle, and cancelling stops the next renewal.

Can I use the data in a product I sell?

Yes, on any paid plan. A commercial license is written into the Terms, so you can show prices to your users, build analytics on top of the data, and cache it server-side. Read the commercial use guidelines for the boundaries.
