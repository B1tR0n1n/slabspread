# Opening a Pack

How to open your pack:

1. Create an account on Courtyard.io if you don't already have one
2. Visit 'My collection', and click on the pack you want to open&#x20;
3. Hit the 'Open pack' button - you'll be asked to confirm once more on the next screen
4. That's it! Sit back, see what you got, and flex your pull on socials

Note: if your pack is in an external wallet, you'll need to first link it to your account under [settings](https://courtyard.io/settings).
