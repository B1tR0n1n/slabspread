# Introduction

Mission Statement

We were tired of slow payments, fraud and more in the web2 collectibles space. With nearly a century of collectibles experience amongst the 3 core team members, we built Collector Crypt, a platform by collectors, for collectors.

We are building the best place to trade collectibles, whether you’re a crypto native or not.

Our backgrounds vary from a CEO former bio-tech entrepreneur with several successful exists, to a CTO native to digital marketing with multiple F500 clients, or a Head of BD with experience across Amazon and Hedge Funds.
