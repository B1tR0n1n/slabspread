# Products

The Bridge:

Every single one of our NFTs is physically redeemable for the real-life, tangible, RWA trading card. You can submit your cards to get the corresponding NFT, or burn the NFT to get the physical card shipped to your door.

Marketplace:
We built a marketplace from the ground up, tailored for trading card UI. It has all the advantages and filters you would expect for TCG’s, with liquidity powered by Magic Eden’s API on Solana and OpenSea on EVM networks, indexing collectibles across chains. 2% royalties + the marketplace fee.

Gacha:

Our Gacha is the highest E(V) digital repack product in existence. Spend $50 or $250 USDC for a random on-chain Pokemon card, then keep it or sell it back to us on the spot for 85% of its insured value. 0 transaction fees.

Bidder:
Our eBay bidder is the world’s first on-ramp for RWA’s to Solana. Place your maximum bid, submit a deposit, then we’ll bid for you and tokenise your trading card for you. 1% success fee or 100% of your money back.

Card Club:

Our premier NFT pass, giving holders exclusive access to whitelists, new products, and more.
