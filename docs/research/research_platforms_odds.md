# Verification spike: vault/tokenized graded-card platforms and published pack odds

Date: 2026-09-25. Method: WebSearch (budget of 200 calls exhausted), WebFetch (most primary hosts blocked by egress proxy), GitHub code search + raw.githubusercontent.com fetches (the only reliably reachable primary-ish sources). Evidence tags per claim:

- **VERIFIED-BY-FETCH** = I fetched the page/file and read it.
- **SEARCH-SNIPPET-ONLY** = only a search-engine snippet of the page was available; the source itself was not fetched.
- **UNKNOWN** = could not find evidence either way.

Important caveat: almost all first-party sites (phygitals.com, docs.phygitals.com, courtyard.io, docs.courtyard.io, collectorcrypt.com, docs.collectorcrypt.com, alt.xyz, arenaclub, beezie docs, etc.) were EGRESS_BLOCKED. Terms-of-service quotations below are therefore from search snippets unless marked otherwise, and should be re-read from the primary page before anything is relied on legally. Third-party GitHub repos that reverse-engineered these APIs were fetchable and are the strongest evidence I have for endpoint shapes and on-chain addresses; treat them as "credible but unofficial".

### 2.4 Legal / regulatory considerations for a published "house edge" report

- **Platform self-characterisation**: Phygitals: "Purchasing packs involves an inherent element of chance… While Phygitals does not intend for pack openings to constitute gambling and does not market them as such, regulatory definitions vary by jurisdiction"; maintains a "Restricted Jurisdictions" page; Pack Parties "not intended to constitute gambling, lottery, or sweepstakes". Collector Crypt calls Gacha a "gamified shopping experience" with claimed positive EV, yet geo-blocks US/UK/China over "gambling-adjacent concerns". Courtyard frames packs as retail with published odds. (SEARCH-SNIPPET-ONLY: docs.phygitals.com pack-opening page; datawallet; bitquery.)
- **Age gates**: Courtyard 18+ (or age of contractual capacity); Phygitals 18+ / age of majority, 48 US states; Collector Crypt 18+ in TON ToS, and review sites cite 21+ / state minimum gambling age for CC. A house-edge report should carry an 18+/21+ notice and should not link to sign-up flows in restricted jurisdictions. (SEARCH-SNIPPET-ONLY.)
- **US**: as of Sep 2026 no US state or federal law regulates loot-box sales or requires odds disclosure; every bill since Hawaii 2018 has died. FTC has acted case-by-case on deceptive practices and issued 2024 warning letters on skin-gambling operators (age verification, unclear odds). Mystery boxes are legal in 48 states; Washington is the statutory ban; Idaho has no operator coverage. (SEARCH-SNIPPET-ONLY: https://shattered.io/loot-box-odds-disclosure-laws-2026/ , https://tech-insider.org/mystery-boxes/are-mystery-boxes-legal-in-the-usa/ , https://natlawreview.com/article/legality-loot-boxes-primer)
- **International**: Belgium bans paid loot boxes; Netherlands applies gambling law to some mystery-box mechanics; Germany requires age-gating (JuSchG); Korea/China/Japan mandate probability disclosure (Korea: treble damages). Payment processors in 2026 ask for odds-disclosure evidence and age-gate proof. (SEARCH-SNIPPET-ONLY: https://track360.io/blog/mystery-box-gambling-or-shopping-operator-compliance-guide , https://dozypay.com/loot-boxes-microtransactions-payment-regulation-2026/)
- **Implications for the report**: (1) describe products in the platforms' own terms ("packs", "gacha", "claw") and state that classification varies by jurisdiction rather than asserting "gambling"; (2) distinguish *published tier odds* from *observed/derived odds* and from *EV under buyback vs. under FMV* (CC and Arena Club value buybacks against their own internal valuations, so "house edge" depends on which FMV oracle you use — note this explicitly); (3) show methodology and data provenance (on-chain tx ranges, API snapshot timestamps) — Bitquery's and DefiLlama's methods are good templates; (4) include 18+ notice and responsible-play language; (5) avoid affiliate links from pages that publish EV/house-edge numbers unless disclosed (FTC endorsement rules; UK/EU affiliate-copy accuracy expectations) — note that PackSpy/PackRipping/RipRanks are affiliate sites, which is exactly the conflict a neutral report should avoid or disclose; (6) if platform ToS forbid automated collection, state that odds were obtained from on-chain data or from the platform's public API/docs, and keep manual page reads dated.
