## Rules of thumb until a row says otherwise
- Public blockchain data: ingest freely; label the chain and contract.
- Licensed price APIs: private dashboard may display raw values; public pages show derived numbers only.
- eBay Browse API: display per eBay API License Agreement; sold/completed data is out of scope entirely.
- Anything with an unresolved question: **excluded**, and the question is logged below.
